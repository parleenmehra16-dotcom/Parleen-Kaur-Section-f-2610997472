Famous Places

1. Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum located in Agra, known worldwide for its architecture.

2. Red Fort

A historic fort in Delhi that served as the main residence of Mughal Emperors.

3. Hawa Mahal

A palace in Jaipur made with red and pink sandstone.

4. Gateway of India

An arch monument built in the early 20th century in Mumbai.

5. Golden Temple

A central religious place of Sikhism located in Amritsar.
